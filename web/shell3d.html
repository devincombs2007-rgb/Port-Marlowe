    Titanic: The Final Hours

    A real-time 3D reconstruction, minute by minute, from the lookout's bell at 23:39 to Carpathia's rescue at dawn. Every event runs on the ship's own clock, one second per second unless you change the speed: the 2 h 40 min sinking, the 20 lifeboats, the break-up, and the six-minute fall to the seabed 3,800 m down.

    Sound on recommended. Time runs at 1× by default; slow motion, fast-forward, lenses and a hidden interface (H) are in the bottom bar. Deep-sea scenes are lit as if by a survey submersible; in reality it is pitch black below 250 m. 2D physics sandbox · Physics lab
