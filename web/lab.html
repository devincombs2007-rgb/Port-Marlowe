Port Marlowe · Physics Lab

Proving the fundamentals

Before the sinking is driven by the ship's own condition, each physical system is proven on its own. Every scenario runs in fixed 1/240 s steps, so the outcome does not depend on your screen's frame rate, and is checked against textbook results. The same checks run headless with npm run test:physics.

Back to Titanic: The Final Hours

Two connected tanks 1 · Conservation & momentum

Water flows through a 0.3 m² opening driven by the pressure difference, with inertia: it overshoots and oscillates like a U-tube before settling at V/(S₁+S₂).

    Sealed vs vented tank 2 · Air pressure

    Three identical tanks 1–4 m below the surface flood through the same inlet. Trapped air compresses (Boyle's law) and stops the sealed tank; a 1 cm² vent lets the air out slowly.

      Tilting part-filled containers 3 · Free surface

      The same volume of water in a broad shallow pool and a narrow deep tank. As the containers heel, the surface stays level and the water's centre of gravity moves toward the low side, far more in the broad pool. A sudden jolt at 25 s starts sloshing.

        Pressure across a hinged door 4 · Force, torque, latch

        A cabin door (0.76 × 1.98 m) between a flooding room and a dry one. The pressure difference is integrated over the leaf; the latch fails when its share of the torque exceeds 2.2 kN, and opening the door lets the rooms equalise. If the door opens into the flooding room, the frame holds it shut.

          Lowering a loaded boat unevenly 5 · Ropes & pulleys

          A 30 ft lifeboat with 40 people hangs from two 3-part manila falls. The forward fall is paid out faster, so the boat tilts and the load redistributes; then passengers crowd aft; then she is lowered to the sea, where buoyancy takes the weight and the ropes go slack.

            Frame-rate independence 6 · Repeatability & convergence

            A combined scenario (tanks, door and boat) is run at 24, 60 and 144 fps and with a jittery 15–120 fps, all through the same fixed-step clock. The state hashes must match bit for bit. Halving the step must shrink the error.
