The story

What the game is about

Rockstar's official synopsis, then what each line tells us.

"Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. But when an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida — forced to rely on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive."

Rockstar Games, official description on its site and store pages Official
  • The deck is stacked against them

    Both leads start from the bottom. Jason grew up around grifters and drifted from the Army into drug-running work in the Keys. Lucia fought for her family and ended up in prison. Official

  • An easy score goes wrong

    A botched job sets everything off. Fans often guess it's a bank job, but Rockstar hasn't said what the score is. Rumor

  • The darkest side of the sunniest place

    Leonida is Florida, and the story lives in its underbelly: smuggling, clubs, crooked money and the police. Official

  • A conspiracy stretching across the state

    The plot moves beyond Vice City into the other regions. The Extended Look already moves between the Keys, the city and the skyline. Seen

  • Rely on each other

    Trust is the theme, from Trailer 1's last line to the switching system. Previews say relationships shape the story, though they don't give gameplay bonuses. Reported

Behind the scenes

Making the biggest game around

The studio, the money, the leaks and the disputes.

Who's making it Official

Rockstar Games, with Edinburgh's Rockstar North leading and Rockstar's other studios contributing. Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson was the face of the 2026 previews. The publisher is Take-Two Interactive, led by CEO Strauss Zelnick.

The 2022 leak Reported

On September 18, 2022, more than 90 clips of early test footage leaked online. Rockstar confirmed they were real and said development would continue. The hacker behind it, a teenager linked to the Lapsus$ group, was later given an indefinite hospital order by a UK court.

Record breaker Official

Trailer 1 holds three Guinness World Records from its first day, including the most-viewed game reveal on YouTube in 24 hours, with 90.4 million views. Rockstar says Trailer 2 reached 475 million views across platforms in 24 hours.

Two delays Official

On May 2, 2025, Rockstar moved the game from fall 2025 to May 26, 2026. On November 6, 2025, it moved again to November 19, 2026, saying it needed more time to polish. Take-Two has repeated the date in every earnings report since.

The firings dispute Reported

At the end of October 2025, Rockstar fired about 30 to 40 staff in the UK and Canada for "gross misconduct," saying they had shared confidential information in a public forum. The IWGB union calls it union-busting over a private union Discord. Legal claims followed, and an early UK ruling in January 2026 found the union hadn't yet proven its case. The dispute continues.

A Netflix premiere Official

The Extended Look debuted on Netflix six hours before it reached YouTube. It was an unusual way to reveal a game.

The money on the line Reported

Analysts note that Take-Two's forecast, reportedly around $8 billion in bookings, is built around the November date. That's one reason another delay is unlikely.

Thirteen years between games Official

GTA V launched on September 17, 2013. GTA VI arrives more than 13 years later, the longest gap between numbered entries in the series.
