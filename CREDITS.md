# Credits and licenses

## THE HOLLOW: Survival Horror
Design, code, models, textures, animations, structures and journal text were made for this project.
Models and textures are generated by the code in `tools/`. The **current audio uses downloaded
CC0 recordings**, processed and layered by `tools/sounds/sourced.py`; it replaces an older
all-synthesis prototype. The source inventory is retained in
[`tools/sounds/sources_used.json`](tools/sounds/sources_used.json), and individual sound recipes
in [`tools/sounds/catalog_generated.json`](tools/sounds/catalog_generated.json).

### Audio sources and processing

The current resource pack contains **150 OGG files**, used by **77 sound events**. The event
catalogue has 161 file references: 150 references to these packaged files and 11 references to
Minecraft's own sheep sounds. Vanilla sounds are referenced, not redistributed.

| Source | Use | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| Kenney Impact Sounds and RPG Audio | Footsteps, creaks, book and equipment sounds | CC0 packs in the sound collection linked below |
| Abstraction / Tallbeard Studios (Benjamin Burnes) | Organic foley, pads and music | Source paths and track names retained in `sources_used.json` |
| rubberduck's CC0 packs on OpenGameArt | Creature, wood, stone, mud and water layers | CC0 packs in the sound collection linked below |
| isaiah658 | “Unsolved Investigation” (night music) | CC0 music collection linked below |
| yd | “Shrine” (shrine music) | CC0 music collection linked below |

Recorded SFX come from [lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds](https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds),
pinned to commit `f2b6264f9ab89fabc266914c3654685d68c5a39b`. Its root license is CC0 1.0.
Music comes from [jfpx/cc0-media-library, music-v1](https://github.com/jfpx/cc0-media-library/releases/tag/music-v1);
the download script verifies archive SHA-256 `f76258c87efa52cb47c7d716830088049226752f97c56fcda830ab43037172cc`.

Processing includes varispeed pitch shifts, layering, filtering, fades, reverb, level adjustment
and encoding. These are adapted recordings, not claims of original performances. The historical
synthesis generator remains in `tools/sounds/design.py` for reference and event calibration.
The source collections' creator declarations are provenance; no endorsement by their creators is implied.

Audio has not received a human listening approval for this add-on. Measurements and browser
playback checks do not replace listening in Minecraft. Use the portable Sound Booth and export
its feedback for retuning.

### Art disclosure
Models and textures are original, built from parametric shapes and procedural painting at Minecraft's texel scale.
Preview images in `docs/previews/` are offline software renders, not in-game screenshots.
