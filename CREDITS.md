## THE HOLLOW: Survival Horror
Design, code, models, textures, animations, sounds, structures, journal text and documentation were made for this
project. The art and audio are **generated by the code in `tools/`**: models and textures are painted procedurally,
and sounds are synthesised from scratch with numpy/scipy. **No external samples, textures, models, fonts or voice
recordings are used.**

### Audio disclosure
All 153 sounds are procedural synthesis: modal synthesis, stick-slip friction, granular crackle, Minnaert bubbles,
formant voices, filtered noise and convolution reverb (`tools/hollowkit/audio.py`, `tools/sounds/design.py`).
They were checked by spectrogram, level and clipping analysis **but have not been listened to by a person** and are
not professionally mastered. They are an original first pass, not finished studio sound design.

### Art disclosure
Models and textures are original, built from parametric shapes and procedural painting at Minecraft's texel scale.
Preview images in `docs/previews/` are offline software renders, not in-game screenshots.
