# THE HOLLOW: Survival Guide

*Ranger Marlowe's notes, made practical.* Everything here is also in your **Field Journal**,
which unlocks each entry as you discover it. This guide spoils all of it.

---

## The rules of this forest

1. **The first day is safe.** Nothing hunts you on day 1. Each player who joins a world also gets about **10 minutes of
   play** before anything can target them, and **one minute of mercy after every respawn**. No spawn kills.
2. **You are watched long before you are attacked.** Every creature has signs you can see or hear
   first, and every one has a counter.
3. **Shelter works.** A roof and a closed door keep you safe. Nothing opens doors, and nothing
   spawns inside a lit, roofed room.
4. **Daytime stays useful.** The Watcher, Knocker and revealed Borrowed are night threats. Caves and
   swamps stay dangerous by day, but you can see them coming.
5. **Sleep stays safe.** You can always sleep. If you hear knocking from bed, it's harmless. Stay in bed.
6. **Nothing destroys your base** unless an operator deliberately turns on *Destructive Knocker*,
   and even then it can only try wooden doors, on Hard.

## Field conditions and next steps

Hold the **Field Journal**, press **Use**, and choose **Field conditions & next steps**.
The page checks your roof, held/nearby light, movement noise and registered protective equipment.
It also explains quiet pacing such as a new arrival's settling-in period or Peaceful mode.
These local checks do not guarantee safety or reveal nearby creatures. Choose **Refresh** after
moving or changing equipment; the page is a snapshot rather than a live background scanner.

The suggested next step follows your discoveries and carried equipment. **Read suggested recipe**
opens a recipe you already unlocked; undiscovered creature/boss recipes stay hidden. The existing
Settings button remains in the same place. Multiple discoveries close together produce one
notification and one sound, and the individual unread pages remain marked in their chapters.

An overflow Rowan Ward is inactive at the world's 32-ward cap. Remove a registered ward, then
use the inactive ward to register it. Inactive wards and failed recharge attempts spend no charcoal.
Death and dimension travel cancel queued item uses and clear lens reveals/Heartseeker markers;
use the tool again when you are ready. Inventory-full rewards drop beside the player.

## How the dread ramps up

Days are counted from when the add-on was first enabled in the world, so an old world gets
the same gentle ramp as a new one.

| When | What can happen |
|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Quiet. Unusual sounds. At dusk you might glimpse a pale face far away in the trees. It never approaches. |
| **Day 2+** | The Watcher stalks at dusk and night in forests. A Borrowed may hide in a sheep flock. Hollowed hunt in **deep** caves (below Y 0). |
| **Day 3+** | Mirebound lurk in swamps, mangroves, rivers and beaches. |
| **Day 4+** | Hollowed also roam shallower caves (below Y 40). |
| **Night 4+** | The Knocker may visit an occupied shelter. Rare: at most one visit in the whole world per night, and at least 2 nights apart on Standard. |
| **When you choose** | Craft the Offering and wake the Heart Below. |

Operators can change the pacing in **Journal → Settings → World rules** (see Settings below).

## The progression loop

```
 signs ─▶ investigate ─▶ materials + journal entries ─▶ craft tools ─▶ dangerous places ─▶ wake the boss ─▶ rewards
```

1. **Survive the first days.** Build a shelter with a door. Craft a **Hollow Chime** and a **Rowan Ward**.
   Both recipes are in your journal from the start.
2. **Learn each creature.** Meeting one unlocks its journal pages. Its counterplay earns you its **material**:
   | Creature | Material | Main way to get it |
   |---|---|---|
   | Watcher | **Pale Resin** | Stare it down: it leaves resin where it stood (1 per night; 2 on Nightmare). Killing it drops 1–2. |
   | Borrowed | **Borrowed Hide** | Expose it and kill it (1–2). |
   | Hollowed | **Hollow Fang** | Kill one (1 each). |
   | Knocker | **Knocker's Nail** | Don't answer: it leaves one on the step. Killing it drops 2–3. |
   | Mirebound | **Mire Pearl** | Kill it (1). |
3. **Explore the places.** **Ranger cabins** in forests and **ruined campsites** in the open appear
   56–80 blocks from players exploring away from spawn. Their chests hold supplies, **Torn Pages**
   (story) and sometimes creature materials.
4. **Craft the Hollow Lens** (Pale Resin) to investigate, then the **Heartseeker** (Pale Resin + Fangs)
   to find the shrine.
5. **Craft the Offering of Silence** (one of each material), go down to the **Underground Shrine** and
   place it in the altar.
6. **Still the Heart Below.** Each participant receives a **Stilled Heart**, which makes a
   **Heartwood Lantern**: permanent protection for your home.

> **No lock-outs.** If an operator turns a creature off, every *new* cabin and campsite chest
> contains that creature's material, so the boss stays reachable. The exception is when sites are turned off too.

## Equipment and recipes

All recipes use a **crafting table**, and every ingredient is obtainable in survival.

### Field Journal (given on first join)
*Shapeless: Book + Charcoal.*
You get one automatically on first join. It's **kept on death** and locked to your inventory, so you can't lose it.
If it's ever missing, you get a new one on respawn (or craft one).
Hold it and press **Use**: chapters are *Field Notes*, *Threats*, *Equipment* and *Places*, plus **Settings**.
New entries are marked **(new)**. It works fully with a controller.

### Hollow Chime: warning device
```
  .      String       .
Nugget   Copper Ingot  Nugget
  .      Iron Nugget   .
```
- Hang it under a ceiling, a beam or a porch roof.
- It **rings when any Hollow creature comes within 14 blocks**, checked every 2 s, with a 6 s cooldown per chime.
- **Use** it to silence or unsilence it.
- No fuel. Up to **32** active chimes per world; extra chimes stay silent and tell you so.
- It only works while a player is within 64 blocks.

### Rowan Ward: limited protection
```
  .      String    .
Nugget   Charcoal  Nugget
Nugget   Stick     Nugget
```
- Place it near your door. **Within 10 blocks:** the Knocker turns back before it knocks, the Watcher won't
  step closer, and a Borrowed is revealed and flees.
- **4 charges.** It spends one charge each time it turns something away, but at most **one charge per creature
  type per night**, even across a save/restart, so one persistent visitor can't drain it.
- **Recharge:** use **Charcoal** on it, +1 charge each. **Use** it with an empty hand to see its charges.
- Breaking a fully charged ward returns it. Breaking a used ward drops salvage (2 nuggets + string), so you can't
  refill one by breaking and re-placing it.
- It does **not** stop the Hollowed, the Mirebound or the Heart Below.
- Up to 32 active wards per world.

### Hollow Lens: investigation tool
```
  .      Copper Ingot  .
Copper   Glass Pane    Copper
  .      Pale Resin    .
```
**Use** it to:
- **reveal every Borrowed within 24 blocks** for 10 s (only *you* see it; other players aren't spoiled);
- read the **direction, distance and height** of the nearest Hollow creature within 48 blocks;
- see **how loud you are** right now to the Hollowed.

It has **64 uses** and a 3 s cooldown, and you **repair it with Copper Ingots** at an anvil (16 uses per ingot).

### Heartseeker: boss progression
*Shapeless: Compass + Pale Resin + 2 Hollow Fangs.*
**Use** it to feel the shrine's direction, distance and depth. It also adds a red star to your
**locator bar**. If no shrine exists yet, the first use forms one **32–48 blocks away, deep below**
(about 40 blocks under you where the world is deep enough, never above Y −12), and never in water, lava or
anything built. Works in the Overworld only, with a 2 s cooldown.

### Offering of Silence: wakes the boss
```
Fang           Pale Resin   Fang
Borrowed Hide  Gold Ingot   Knocker's Nail
  .            Mire Pearl   .
```
Place it in the **altar** at the centre of the shrine.

### Heartwood Lantern: the reward
*Shapeless: Stilled Heart + Lantern.*
A warm, permanent light. **Within 24 blocks** no Hollow creature spawns or is drawn in, and the
Knocker never visits. This protects ordinary encounters; a boss fight you deliberately awaken,
including its summoned minions, still runs. Up to 16 active lanterns per world; extra lanterns
show a message and provide light only.

## The creatures

### The Watcher (forests, dusk and night)
- **Signs:** the woods go quiet (a hush), the fog thickens a little, and a **pale face at the treeline**,
  34–50 blocks away. It always appears out of your view, so it's *there* when you turn; it never pops in.
- **Rule:** it **only moves while nobody is looking at it.**
- **What happens if you ignore it:** after ~3 s unwatched it creeps closer, tree by tree. Within 10 blocks you
  hear a **twig snap behind you**: that's your warning. Within 5 blocks, in the dark, it lunges for one strike, then leaves.
- **Counterplay:**
  - **Look at it** for about a second and it withdraws, leaving **Pale Resin**.
  - **Hold a torch or lantern**: it won't strike you.
  - Stand near placed light; it won't approach a lit place.
  - A charged **Rowan Ward** holds it back.
  - It leaves at dawn, after 3 minutes, or if you go 96+ blocks away. On *Calm* it never strikes.

### The Borrowed (sheep flocks)
- **Signs:** one sheep that **never grazes**, stands still while the flock wanders, **faces you**, and has a bleat
  that is a little too low and **ends in a click**. At night its eyes catch the light.
- **Tests:** hold **wheat** (real sheep come, it doesn't); try to **shear** it (the wool peels instead);
  look through the **Hollow Lens**.
- **Rule:** disguised, it's harmless. It's revealed by shears, by being hurt, by a ward, or **at night if you stand within
  4 blocks of it in the dark for 2 s without a light**. Revealed at night, it hunts; by day it only flees.
  It flees below 40% health.
- **Counterplay:** expose it by day, or at night with a light in hand and your back to a wall. It only joins flocks of
  2+ sheep, so no flock means no Borrowed.

### The Hollowed (deep caves)
- **Signs:** **rapid echolocation clicks**, distant breathing, scraping on stone. It's blind.
- **It hears** (with the radius in blocks):
  | Noise | Radius | | Noise | Radius |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Sprinting | 16 | | Doors, trapdoors, gates, chests, barrels | 10 |
  | Walking | 6 | | Levers, buttons, pressure plates, tripwire, pistons | 10 |
  | Jumping / landing | 8 | | Fighting (any hurt involving a player) | 14 |
  | Breaking a block | 12 | | Explosions | 40 |
  | Placing a block | 8 | | Thrown things landing (snowball, egg, arrow…) | 12 |
- **It can't hear:** **sneaking**, standing still, or walking on **wool or carpet**.
- **Within 3 blocks it finds you anyway.**
- **Counterplay:** crouch past it. **Throw something** away from you: it goes to the sound. Keep light around;
  it only emerges from darkness, out of your line of sight. Fight in the open, not in a tunnel.
- Limits: at most 2 near you (3 on Nightmare) and 6 in the world.

### The Knocker (night visits to occupied shelters)
- **Signs:** your **chime rings**, then **three knocks** at the door, scratching, and breathing against the wood. About 45 seconds in all.
- **Rule:** it only visits a player who is **inside a roofed shelter with a door**, never before night 4, rarely, and
  never where a Heartwood Lantern burns. **It can't open doors.**
- **Counterplay:**
  - **Don't answer.** It gives up, leaves, and drops a **Knocker's Nail** on the step.
  - A charged **Rowan Ward** within 10 blocks of the door sends it away before it knocks.
  - If you *do* open the door, it comes in. It's slow but strong (60 HP). Fight it in the light, back through a
    doorway, or shut the door between you. It leaves after 90 s, when badly hurt, or at daybreak.
- *Destructive Knocker* (operator option, off by default) lets it try to break a **wooden** door late in a visit,
  and only on **Hard**. Iron doors always hold.

### The Mirebound (swamps, mangroves, rivers, beaches)
- **Signs:** a **mossy hump with reeds** in 1–2 block deep water that wasn't there before, **bubbles**, a
  **low gurgle** when you're near, and **dull amber eyes** just under the surface at night.
- **Rule:** if you wade within ~3.5 blocks, it **rears up**. A 0.6 s windup is your escape window.
  If you're still in reach, it grabs you and pulls you under (2 damage every 1.5 s).
- **Counterplay:** **walk the banks, not the shallows.** If grabbed, **press Sneak** to wrench free,
  or hit it. It lets go after **5 s** at most and grabs at most twice per encounter. It never follows you onto land.

### The Heart Below (the boss, only when you wake it)
See the next section.

## Waking the Heart Below

**Prepare:** full armour, food, a bow, blocks to pillar up, torches, and **friends** (it scales fairly).
Bring a way back to the surface.

1. Follow the **Heartseeker** down to the shrine: a root-choked chamber with four pillars and an altar.
2. **Use** the altar with the **Offering of Silence**. The room darkens, and a few seconds later the Heart rises from the roots.
3. **Phase 1 (100–60%).** The Heart is **shielded** while its **four veins** glow on the pillars. **Break every vein**,
   then hit the Heart.
   - **Root bursts:** dust and a rumble mark the ground under you for 1.2 s, then roots erupt (6 damage).
     Step off the dust.
   - **Pulse ring:** a slow expanding ring along the floor (5 damage). **Jump** over it. It only hits you if you're on the ground.
4. **Phase 2 (60–25%).** The shield returns and **three veins regrow** (once). **Young Hollowed** crawl out; they
   hunt by sound, so kill them first. The bursts come faster.
5. **Phase 3 (below 25%).** It **opens**. Bursts come faster still, and a **sweeping gaze** crosses the room
   (3 damage + slowness). **Break line of sight behind a pillar.**

**Scaling:** with *Scale the Heart Below* on, health is 300 / 480 / 660 / 840 for 1 / 2 / 3 / 4+ players
in the arena at awakening. There is one root burst per player, minions number 2 + players (max 5), and veins are
tougher with 3+ players.

**Rewards:** everyone who dealt damage or was in the arena at the end gets **one Stilled Heart** and **150 XP**,
exactly once per kill. The Heart also drops diamonds (2–4), gold (4–8), a golden apple and bottles o' enchanting
(3–6). The altar then **rests for 3 days**, and a new fight needs a new Offering.

**Bailing out:** if everyone leaves the chamber for **60 s**, the Heart goes dormant and **returns the Offering**
to the altar. Nothing is lost.

## Settings

Open **Journal → Settings**.

### My comfort settings (every player, saved per player)
| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Ambience volume | 80% | Volume of the add-on's ambient cues (wind, branch snaps, cave drips, distant calls). |
| Encounter accents | Subtle | Short musical stingers when something is noticed. Off / Subtle / Full. |
| Visual effects | On | Encounter fog shifts and brief soft fades. There's no camera shake and no flashes in any mode. |
| Action-bar hints | On | One-line explanations of what just happened ("a twig snaps behind you"). |

### World rules (operators only: Realm owner and operators)
| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Horror intensity | Standard | **Calm:** rarer, and the Watcher never strikes. **Nightmare:** more frequent, bolder, shorter cooldowns. |
| Encounter frequency | 100% | 25–200%, scales spawn chances and cooldowns. |
| Grace days | 1 | Days before creatures hunt (0–10). |
| First Knocker night | 4 | 1–20. |
| Creature toggles | all on | Turn any of the five creatures off. Their materials then appear in new site chests. |
| Destructive Knocker | **off** | May try to break wooden doors, on Hard only. |
| Rare bedtime knocking | on | A harmless knock while you sleep, about one night in six after the Knocker's first night. Never blocks sleep. |
| Place sites | on | Cabins, campsites and the shrine. |
| Scale the Heart Below | on | Boss health and pressure scale with player count. |

### What Minecraft controls (not the add-on)
Minecraft's own **Settings → Video** controls Graphics Mode (Vibrant Visuals or standard), render distance,
brightness, field of view and camera settings. **Settings → Audio** controls Master, Music, Sound, *Ambient/Environment*,
*Hostile Creatures* and other category sliders: the add-on's creature sounds follow *Hostile Creatures* (the
disguised Borrowed's bleat follows *Friendly Creatures*), its ambience follows *Ambient/Environment*, and chimes and
wards follow *Blocks*. Difficulty (Peaceful/Easy/Normal/Hard) scales creature damage as in vanilla.
On **Peaceful** the add-on's creatures don't spawn.

## Multiplayer notes
- Encounters are **per player**. Each player has their own cooldowns, and limits keep the world total low.
- Journal progress, comfort settings and play time are **saved per player**. Sites, the boss state and world rules are
  **saved in the world** and survive restarts.
- Discovering a site or seeing a creature unlocks the entry for whoever was there.
- Leaving mid-encounter cleans up that encounter. Rejoining starts fresh, with nothing duplicated.
- Boss rewards go to each participant once. Participants must be online when the Heart dies.
