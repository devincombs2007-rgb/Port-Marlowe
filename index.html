How we got here

  1. Feb 4, 2022

    Rockstar confirms it

    A Rockstar Newswire post says active development on the next Grand Theft Auto is well underway.

  2. Sep 18, 2022

    The leak

    More than 90 clips of early test footage spread online. Rockstar confirms they are genuine and says work continues.

  3. Dec 4, 2023

    Trailer 1

    Posted a day early after it leaked. It introduces Lucia, the state of Leonida and Vice City, with a 2025 target.

  4. May 2, 2025

    First delay

    Launch moves from fall 2025 to May 26, 2026.

  5. May 6, 2025

    Trailer 2

    Jason and Lucia take the spotlight, with a wave of screenshots and a rebuilt official site.

  6. Nov 6, 2025

    Second delay

    Rockstar asks for more time to polish and sets Thursday, November 19, 2026.

  7. Nov 19, 2026

    Launch 13 days to go

    Grand Theft Auto VI arrives on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
