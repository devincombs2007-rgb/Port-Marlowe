# Port-Marlowe

IMP4EVR is a single-file app for training, nutrition, focus, sleep, habits and goals. Everything lives in `index.html`.

Open `index.html` in a browser to use it. Data stays on the device. Inside a Claude artifact it also syncs to your Claude account and Autopilot (the built-in coach) becomes available.

## The 8.1 look

Version 8.1 restyles the whole app: a condensed scoreboard typeface for every headline and big number (embedded, so it works offline), flat panels with hairline edges, a floating tab dock, a slanted segment meter on the charge card, and saved images set in the same type. Light and dark themes both follow it.

## What's in version 8

- **Player card.** An overall rating from 40 to 99, built from six attributes (Strength, Engine, Fuel, Focus, Recovery, Discipline) over your last 28 days. It has tiers from Rookie to Legend, an archetype, a card you can tilt and save as an image, and a rank-up screen when you reach a new tier.
- **Scouting report.** Strengths, development areas and the single change worth the most rating points. It is written on the device, and Autopilot can write a deeper one.
- **Daily quests.** Three a day, aimed at your weakest attributes, with bonus XP and a sweep bonus for all three.
- **Command bar.** Press `/` or Ctrl/Cmd+K, or tap the bar on Today. Type what you did ("3 eggs and toast", "slept 7.5", "ran 30", "weight 172", "task call mom tomorrow") or where you want to go ("plates", "recap").
- **Live core.** The charge card glows with the four ring colors and brightens as the day fills up. Turn it off under You → Settings → Live visuals.
- **Autopilot model.** Autopilot asks for Claude Fable 5.2 by default. Change it under You → Settings → Autopilot model.

Everything from 7.4 is still there: workouts, the food scanner, focus timer, sleep mode, recap stories, goals, challenges, widgets, Drive and TV modes, and connections.
